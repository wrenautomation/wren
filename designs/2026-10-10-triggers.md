# Triggers: news that makes a firm worth writing now (2026-10-10)

## Answer first

A firm that just bought another, merged, raised, opened an office or named a leader has a reason to hear from us this month. The `triggers` pool stage reads Google News for each of a niche's trigger words, has a model name the niche firm in each headline, and adds that firm as a lead with the news as its first finding. A firm we already hold gets the news as a dated finding instead. Free: RSS plus one model call per 25 headlines.

Before this, news was read only for firms we already held (the `news` signal collector, reactivation's company-events check). Nothing found new firms from news.

## Measured

One RSS search per word, last 14 days, 2026-10-10:

| Word | Items | Firm news in the top 5 |
|---|---|---|
| staffing agency | 7 | 1 |
| recruiting firm | 6 | 2 (one firm, two outlets) |
| marketing agency | 48 | 4 |

Recruiting is thin and noisy; agencies is rich. The model drops headlines about no niche firm (a school program, a broker's advice), so the noise costs a model call, not a bad lead.

## How it runs

- Stage `triggers` in `PoolScheduler/{niche}`, after `youtubeSearch`, 3 words a pass. Wren's niches only, and only with a fetcher. `Enrichment/<niche>/triggers` by ingress for a set number.
- A niche lists its words in `triggerWords` (`packages/niches`). Recruiting: staffing agency, staffing firm, recruiting firm, executive search firm. Agencies: marketing, digital and advertising agency.
- A query is the word as a phrase plus the six kinds' words, `when:14d`. A hit counts when it is dated in the window and its title reads as one of the kinds (`eventKindOf`, the same regexes as `searchEvents`).
- Every new headline is kept as a document, so none goes to the model twice.
- A firm we hold is matched by bare name in the niche, exact and unique; else it is imported (source `news_trigger`, key `nt:<bare name>`) with a `news` finding. `discover` looks up `nt:` firms first, since the reason goes stale.
- A word is read again after 7 days. A bucket over `imports` allows 20 reads a day. New firms get the niche's screen.

## Decisions

- News first. Job boards, Reddit and X are next and wait on the same shape (word, read, name, import).
- Client niches are in development: a client's own words and its own pool need the per-client path the other find stages use.
- No paid source. The Google page and Exa stay with the `news` collector for held firms.

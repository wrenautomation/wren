# Inbox → Done

2026-10-11. temp_notes: "when I approve something there should be a place to see my full history of stuff done, especially for the day."

## Decision

History already lives in `draft_events`. Done is a list over it, not a new store.

- View `done_records`: every step by a person or by Claude at their ask (approved, rejected, edited), plus what followed (scheduled, sent, failed). The model's first words and Wren's own drops stay on each draft's Activity tab.
- `day` is computed in the view (today, week, older) by New York's day, since saved views have no relative dates.
- Record `marketing.done` (`packages/content/src/social/done-record.ts`): tabs Today, This week, Approved, Rejected, Sent, All. Quick filters step, site, kind. Group by site works (channel field `platform`).
- Page Inbox → Done, beside To approve. `open` links each row to its item's Marketing page.

## Not done

- Template, document and pay-link approvals: they live in their own version tables, not `draft_events`. Add them to the view when one needs it.

## Renderer (packages/ui/src/metro)

- **Circles only.** A hub is a circle, never a pill. A closed group is a circle with its step count. People show as the sub line.
- **Fold without jitter.** The glide moves in screen px (`unitsOf`), so a fold that changes the scale does not jump.
- **Loops back** ride a dashed rail under the line, with one mark.
- **Empty column.** A column with no station shows a dashed "To map" stop.
- **ELK + BK** stays the layout. Two facts we proved:
  - `elk.alignment: CENTER` lines up a layer by circle centres. Without it, nodes of different widths line up on an edge, and narrow ones sit a slot off.
  - ELK ignores a node's own `elk.margins`. An exit source is made taller instead (as much above as below), so its exit has room and its circle stays on the row.
- **Entry lines** keep the shared out colour. The schema gives entries one tier, on purpose.
- **Fold path.** It shows only when it names a node. Alone, "Map" says nothing.

## Map page (apps/portal/web/src/map/MapPage.tsx)

- The map fills the first screen (`metro-fill`, `bare`: no dot grid). Today scrolls under it.
- Fill rule: the whole map in its frame, at the top, centred across. Never above its real size, never under 60% of it. Past that it pans (a phone, a big shape on a small screen).
- One bar: window, History, depth −/+. Client, export, import and views sit behind ⋯.
- One side panel at a time: History, else the import preview, else the node.
- **History stays on the map.** We planned a separate History route, and dropped it. History, drafts and the import preview draw their diff on this map. A separate page would need a second map with the same marks.
- The focus page (`FocusPage`) drops the dot grid too.

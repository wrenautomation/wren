# Client health (2026-10-07)

William, 2026-10-07: "churn stuff is good." The brief is the churn research of the same day
(Vitally, Hook, PostHog and the rest): a few readable sub-scores, a person's judgment beside the
model, flags that clear themselves, outcomes over activity, every number opening its rows.

## Answer first

- **Health per client, daily.** Four sub-scores from 0 to 100: results against the plan,
  engagement, sentiment, money. Weights are on screen. A missing input hands its weight to the
  others. Each input shows its age, and an old one marks its sub-score stale.
- **A person's call sits beside the model.** An operator's 1 to 5 rating feeds sentiment. An
  override replaces the shown score with a reason and a name, and the model's number stays next
  to it.
- **One flags list.** Risk or opportunity, with an owner, raised, addressed and cleared.
  DeliveryWatch's problems move onto it, and so do health and a client's interest in a next
  offer. A flag clears itself when its cause goes away.
- **Each flag is a spine event.** Raised and cleared fire `trigger.flag`, so a workflow starts
  from one. Anything it sends waits in To approve. Inbox stays inbound only.
- **Few alerts.** One open flag per cause. Urgent ones go out on the next hourly pass,
  the rest in one 09:00 digest grouped by owner. A standing flag nobody addressed comes back
  weekly.
- **Keep, in Reactivation.** A client's current accounts, scored from findings we already
  collect: the champion left, a gap past the usual reorder time, hiring again.
- **History is kept.** One row per client per day, with the inputs behind it, never deleted.

## Where it lives

- `packages/delivery/src/health/` (`@wren/delivery/health`): schema, scoring, inputs, flags,
  the digest, records, the `HealthConsole` service. Health rides DeliveryWatch's hourly pass,
  so it is live on deploy with no new loop to start. Living in delivery avoids a cycle: health
  reads engagements, and the watch writes flags.
- Tables sit in the `delivery` schema in main. `delivery.flags` is a different table from
  `public.flags` (feature flags); the code calls it `clientFlags`.
- Keep is a view in every client database (`reactivation_keep`) and the record
  `reactivation.keep`.

## Scoring

| Sub-score | Default weight | Inputs | Score | Stale after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Results | 40 | the offer's `target` measure against what's expected by now | `min(100, 100 × actual / expected)` | 14 days without a results update |
| Engagement | 20 | days someone on their side visited (30 days), approvals, answers | average of the parts present | never: read live |
| Sentiment | 25 | each person's latest weekly rating, the operator's latest rating | `(rating − 1) × 25`, averaged | 21 days |
| Money | 15 | the oldest unpaid invoice past its due day | none 100, up to 7 days late 60, 8 to 30 days 30, over 30 days 0 | never: read live |

- **Results.** An offer gains an optional `target`: `{ measure: "meetings", count: 20, fromDay: 14 }`
  on reactivation. Expected by day *d* is `count × (d − fromDay) / (days − fromDay)`, up to
  `count`. Under one expected meeting it reads "Too early" and counts as missing. The snapshot
  for the lander drops `target`.
- **Engagement.** Visits: distinct days in the last 30 with a visit (`client_members.last_seen_at`,
  kept daily in history, plus sign-ins in `auth.session`), against 4. Approvals: deliverables
  decided in 60 days against those waiting over 3 days. Answers: asks answered in 60 days
  against those overdue. Nobody able to sign in scores 0.
- **Overall** is `Σ wᵢsᵢ / Σ wᵢ` over the sub-scores present. 70 and up is Healthy, 40 to 69
  Watch, under 40 At risk. Nothing present is No data.
- **Override.** 0 to 100 with a reason, one open per client, kept with who and when. The shown
  score is the override while it stands. Both numbers show everywhere.

## Flags

- A flag: client, engagement (optional), side (risk or opportunity), source (delivery, health,
  person), cause, what, owner, raised, addressed with a note, cleared, and who did each.
- **Cause** is the dedupe key: `quiet`, `step:<key>`, `invoice:<id>`, `health:at-risk`. One open
  flag per client, engagement and cause.
- **Clears itself** when a pass no longer finds its cause. A one-time cause (an interest, a
  quotable review) never clears itself; a person clears it. A person may clear only those, and
  their own; the rest they mark addressed.
- **From health:** `health:at-risk` (band at risk), `health:drop` (down 15 or more in 7 days),
  `health:ahead` (opportunity: results 25% ahead of plan, 3 or more).
- **From delivery:** every DeliveryWatch problem, with its reping days. `delivery.pings` goes.
  An interest in a next offer is an opportunity. A 4 or 5 review that may be quoted is one too.
- **Alerts.** Urgent causes (a client's comment with no reply, a weekly rating of 2 or lower, an
  interest) go out on the next pass, all in one message. The rest wait for the digest at 09:00
  fleet time: new flags, then standing unaddressed ones whose reping time came, grouped by owner
  (none is "Team"). Bills keep their daily reping in the first week. Addressed flags stay quiet.
- **Spine.** `trigger.flag` (event kind `client`): when raised, cleared or either; risk,
  opportunity or either. Subject `flag:<id>`, client null (Wren's own workflows). The pass marks
  each change fired on the row, and the watch sends it to the Spine after the pass is journaled.

## Keep

- A current account is a company with a placement in the last 24 months.
- Its usual gap is the median gap between its distinct placement dates. With fewer than two, the
  client's median across accounts; with none, 180 days.
- Signals, first match wins: champion left (the person behind the latest placement moved or
  left), past the usual gap by 25%, hiring again (open roles found in the last 30 days), news
  in 6 months, else steady.
- Risk score: champion left 50, past the gap 30, no contact in 6 months 20. Hiring and news are
  the reason to call, not risk.
- Site visits (built 10-09). Our pages keep no visitor id, so a visit is known only when the
  visitor says who they are: a form sent on one of the client's own Sites pages or forms, with a
  known contact's email or an email at an account's domain. The reactivation pass copies the last
  30 days of the client's `site_entry_records` into `account_visits` (once per form). Free mail
  and platform domains never match. Signal `visited`, "Filled <form> on your site", for 30 days:
  a reason to call, not risk.
- Job orders (built 10-09). No ATS connector yet (that's the SaaS connectors item), so they come
  in as a file: `wren --client <id> crm job-orders <file> [--format bullhorn|jobadder|generic]`,
  header synonyms like the CRM formats. Rows land in `job_orders` (the ATS id unique per format),
  matched to a company by domain, else by name; unmatched rows are kept with their company's
  name and counted. Again replaces each row by its id.
  An open order means the account is giving work: Keep doesn't count "past the usual gap" while
  one is open, and with no stronger signal says "2 open job orders" (signal `orders`).
- Signal order now: champion left, overdue, hiring, visited, news, orders, steady.

## Pages

- **Clients → Overview:** At risk, Watch, Open risks, Opportunities, Stale; top lists of the
  lowest scores and the newest flags.
- **Clients → Health:** every client's score, band, the four sub-scores, the override beside it,
  stale marked. A client opens to its inputs (each with age and a link to its rows) and its
  daily history. Rate, Override, Clear override.
- **Clients → Flags:** open, risks, opportunities, cleared; owner is a filter. Take, Assign, Mark
  addressed, Clear, Raise.
- **Clients → a client:** a Health section with the score and its open flags.
- **Wren home, Now:** at-risk clients and urgent open flags above the setups.
- **Reactivation → Keep:** current accounts by risk, with the signal and why.

## CLI

- `wren flags list [--all] [--for <client>]|raise <client> <risk|opportunity> <what>|own <id> [email]|address <id> [--note]|clear <id>`

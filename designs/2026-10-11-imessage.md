# iMessage first

2026-10-11. From William's notes: "facetime / imessage and first class color setup for phones
(imessage and facetime audio >>>> normal text and android)".

## Decision

- **Texts:** Linq as a second texting provider (`packages/channel-sms/src/linq.ts`,
  `WREN_SMS_PROVIDER=linq`, `WREN_LINQ_API_KEY`). Each text goes out from our own Linq line as
  iMessage, else RCS, else SMS (Linq's own fallback). No Mac, no Apple ID of William's.
- **Color everywhere:** every text keeps what carried it (`sms_messages.service`: imessage, rcs,
  sms), from the send answer or a later receipt. The phone app draws outbound bubbles blue
  (iMessage) or green (RCS, SMS); the portal's Texts thread marks each text "by iMessage" with a
  blue or green rule. Everything Telnyx sent is green (backfilled in 0232).
- **Who is blue:** enrollment's lookup becomes Linq's iMessage check, then its RCS check, kept on
  `sms_contacts.imessage`. Neither reaches them: the lead is unknown and passed by. Linq has no
  carrier lookup, so a landline can't be told from an old Android; a text into the dark is worse
  than one lead fewer.
- **Calls stay on Telnyx:** Linq has no API to place a call, FaceTime or not. A Telnyx call webhook
  handed to the Linq provider still parses as Telnyx, so missed calls and text-back keep working.
  FaceTime audio stays by hand from William's iPhone.
- **Webhooks:** `/webhooks/linq` on the phone Worker, Standard Webhooks HMAC against
  `LINQ_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, then `SmsEvents/ingest`. Subscribe with `?version=2026-02-03` (the flat
  payload `parseLinqEvent` reads); an older version is refused loudly.
- **Links:** Linq refuses a link in a chat's first text (code 1005, a protection for the line).
  The provider answers it as a permanent refusal in words, so an opener with a link shows why.
- **Live gate:** `WREN_SMS_LIVE` still holds every send, same as Telnyx.

## Not done, and why

- **The account:** a live Linq line is quoted by sales (reported around $250 a month a line plus
  setup) and the sandbox is a 7-day trial not meant for real leads. Money is William's call, and a
  trial started now would lapse before the cutover. Steps once he says yes: sign up through
  autobrowse, get the key and a line, `secrets` the key into `/wren/prod/env`, set
  `WREN_SMS_PROVIDER=linq`, subscribe the webhook, `wrangler secret put LINQ_WEBHOOK_SECRET`,
  `wren sms numbers sync`.
- **Both at once:** one texting provider a deploy. Splitting by contact (blue on Linq, green on
  Telnyx) means two pools and two sticky senders a phone; Linq's fallback already covers green.

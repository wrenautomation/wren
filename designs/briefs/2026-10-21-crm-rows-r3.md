## Part 1: Overview E

The Inbox Overview becomes E. Other overview pages keep the tiles.

- `OverviewPage` gets `queues?: OverviewQueue[]`. A queue has `label`, `record`, `view`, `href`, `icon` (kit icon name), `line` (0 to 7, drawn as `var(--ui-line-N)`; a queue with no line is `--ui-ink-3`), `wait` (the date field its wait counts from) and `promise` ("24h").
- When a page has `queues`, `RecordOverview` draws E in place of the tile grid:
  - A grid 3fr and 2fr. One column at the narrow breakpoint (`useNarrow`).
  - Left, the hero: the total that waits on you, 48px, then one line "wait on you · N queues late · oldest 2 days". Under it a 10px bar split by queue, each part in its queue's colour, its width by count. Under that, the queues ranked by oldest wait, oldest first. Each queue row: a colour square, the icon, the name over a small "oldest 2 days" line (late in amber text), a count chip and a chevron. The whole row is a link to `href`. A queue with nothing waiting goes to the end, quiet, with "Nothing waiting".
  - Right, the trend cards: the page's `period` tiles (Replies, Booked), each with its value, its sparkline and "+14 vs the 30 days before". Use the trend code that exists in `overview.tsx` (`Tile`, `Trends`); do not write a second chart.
- Data, per queue: one list read of its `view`, sorted by `wait` ascending, limit 1. Its total is the count; its first row's `wait` is the oldest. Read through `recordsApi` (the reads guard allows nothing else). A queue whose record is not installed is left out, as tiles are now.
- Late: a queue is late when its oldest wait is past its promise. Use `lateOf` and `hoursSay` from `@wren/core/promise` (on `rows` already).
- Amber only for late, as text or a dot. Never a fill. The square amber-top tiles go from this page.
- Mail rules leaves the overview (its page stays). The `top` lists stay under E only if they add a fact E does not show. "Waiting on you" and "Mail that needs you" are now queues: drop them from `top`. Keep `CONVERSATION_TOP` if it is not a queue.
- The inbox's queues and their colours (from the artifact): Waiting on you `line 0`, Issues for you `line 2`, To approve `line 1`, Mail that needs you `line 3`, Mentions no line, Asks for access `line 1`. Promise 24h for each. These promises are a default; the planner lists them for William.

Stories on sample data only (`overview.stories.tsx` or a new story file): E with six queues, two late; E with all queues empty; E loading; E on the phone. Play tests: the queues are in oldest-first order; a late queue's oldest line has the late style; a queue row links to its `href`; the total equals the sum of the counts.

# DM door: a systemized way into DMs

2026-10-11. temp_notes: "systemized way to get into DMs".

## Decision

One door per comment, picked in code (`dmDoorOf`, `packages/outreach/src/comments.ts`). "DM them" on any comment goes through it.

| Where the comment is | Door | How |
|---|---|---|
| A reach account's inbox (Reddit) | `reach` | `dmCommenter`: queued for that account's sender, its window and caps |
| Our Instagram or Facebook post | `private_reply` | Meta Private Replies: `POST /{page-id}/messages`, `recipient.comment_id`. One per comment, within 7 days |
| X | none | DMs need theirs open to us: answer in public, ask them to DM |
| LinkedIn | none | no DM from a comment: answer in public; a connect note is the way in |
| YouTube, TikTok | none | no DM API: answer in public with the link |

## Why

- Meta's private reply is the only DM a platform lets a business open from a comment without the person writing first. It fits the 24h window rule: their reply opens the normal window.
- Every DM writes a `reach_messages` row (sent) and a touch, so the personalizer sees it and a second DM is refused.
- A door that doesn't exist refuses with the next best move, so the console never offers a dead button (the record's `door` field gates "DM them").

## Shape

- `ContentChannel.privateReply?(commentId, text)`: Meta's Instagram and Facebook channels; absent elsewhere.
- `Content/privateReply` handler: effect `sends`, 501 where absent.
- `ReachDesk/dmComment`: no account → `planPrivateReply` (sort not ours, door, not DMed, 7 days, not opted out), `Content.privateReply`, `privateReplied` (contact upsert, sent message, comment's `contact_id`, touch).

## Later

- Their answer lands in the Page's DMs (`connect/dms.ts` keeps it by Meta id); tying it to the contact made here by handle is a follow-up.
- Clients' own Pages: the same handler with `client` once their `content.posting` sends are on.

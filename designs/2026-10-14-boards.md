# Boards: a lean kanban inside Notes

2026-10-14. William: a super lean kanban, AI native, that invites discussion; for Wren and for
clients; works with Sheets and Excel instead of replacing them; doesn't fight files and folders.
"Needs to be integrated into wren. It's related to notes, figure out how the UI should be organized."
We tried beads (Steve Yegge's agent tracker) for our own work first; this takes what worked there:
every card has an owner, a status history (when it was given, started, done, reopened), blockers,
and agents read and write it the same way people do.

## The one decision

**A card is a note.** `notes.kind` gains `task`. Everything a note has, a card has: the doc body
(details, plan, checklist), comments and suggest mode (the discussion), @ mentions with mail,
versions, sharing (team, a client, one person), backlinks on every record it @-links, search,
the `wren notes` CLI. The board only adds what a note lacks: a column, an owner, a due date,
blockers, and the history of moves. No second editor, no second comment system.

## Data (every workspace database, so clients get it too)

- `boards`: id, name, columns (ordered list of `{id, name, done: bool}`), owner, general
  (private | workspace), created/archived. Default board "Work" with To do / Doing / Done, made
  on first open. A board can be shared like a note (`note_shares` pattern, `board_shares`).
- `notes` (kind `task`) gains nullable `board_id`, `column`, `position` (fractional index
  string), `assignee` (email or `agent:<name>`), `due` (date), `closed_at`.
- `task_blocks`: task_id, blocked_by (note ids). A card shows "Blocked by" and won't count as
  ready while any blocker is open.
- `task_moves`: task_id, from_column, to_column, by, at, why (optional one line). Written on
  create (from null), every column change, assign, and close/reopen. This is the timeline:
  issued, started, done, iterated, with who and when. Never updated or deleted.

## UI

Notes app pages become: **Notes** (home, as now), **Board**, **Mentions**.

- **Board page.** Columns side by side, cards drag between and within columns (copy
  `modules/deals/board.tsx`: native drag, column picker on phones). A card face: title, owner
  avatar (agents get the wren mark), due (red when past), a blocked dot, comment count, the client
  chip when it @-links one. Top bar: board switcher (Work + others + New board), filters (owner,
  client, label from the note's tags, "blocked", "mine"), a **List** toggle that shows the same
  cards as a `RecordList` (sortable, saved views, CSV export). New card: a "+" at each column's
  foot, title only, Enter adds the next.
- **Card open = the note's doc page**, with a task strip above the title: column (picker),
  owner, due, board, Blocked by / Blocks (note pickers), and a **Timeline** tab beside Versions
  and Comments listing `task_moves` in plain words ("William moved it to Doing, 3 days ago").
- **From a note to cards.** In any note, a checklist line gets a small "Make card" action: it
  makes a task on the default board titled from the line, links it (`note:<id>` link both ways),
  and the line shows the card's column after it. A selected block of lines: "Make cards".
- **Capture.** ⌘K "New card" (title, optional board), and the existing quick capture gets a
  "Card" switch. Ask Claude: "turn this note into cards" proposes cards in To approve style
  (a list you tick), never writes them unasked.
- **Where else cards show.** A record page's backlinks list shows its cards with their column.
  Home's "Needs you" counts cards assigned to you that are due or blocked-on-you.
- **Clients** see the Board page in their Notes when the team shares a board with them
  (`client:<id>`), with their own role; a client can make their own boards in their workspace.

## Agents (AI native)

- Record type `notes.task` (defineRecord, app `notes`): agents list, get and call through
  `/api/agent` with no extra work. Fields: board, column, owner, due, blocked, ready.
- `wren tasks` CLI (beads' verbs): `ready` (open, unblocked, assigned to me or nobody, oldest
  first), `ls [--board --column --owner]`, `show <id>`, `add <title> [--board --column --owner
  --due --blocked-by --body]`, `move <id> <column> [--why]`, `assign <id> <who>`,
  `close <id> --why`, `reopen`, `comment <id> <text>` (a note comment). Writes as
  `agent:claude` (or `--agent`). `--json` everywhere.
- An agent never closes a card owned by a person; it moves it to the board's last non-done
  column ("Review" if present) and comments.

## Sheets and files: work with them, don't replace them

- **CSV and Sheets paste.** Board → Export gives CSV (title, column, owner, due, blocked by,
  link). Import takes a CSV or a pasted table (Sheets/Excel copy is tab separated): map columns
  once, then each row becomes a card; a row whose link matches an existing card updates it.
- **A card can point at a file.** Pasting a Drive, Sheets, Dropbox or OneDrive link into a card
  makes a link chip; a Google Doc/Sheet shows its title (Drive read we already have). Files stay
  where they are; we never copy or reorganize folders.
- **Later, not now:** two-way Google Sheets sync (needs a Sheets connector and the client's
  consent). Noted in Open below.

## Decision log

Changes made while building (2026-10-14).

- `notes.column` is `column_id` in the database (`column` is reserved SQL).
- `task_moves` also stores `kind` (create, move, assign, close, reopen) and `assignee`, so the Timeline reads give and close lines without guessing. Moves keep column ids; names come from the board's current columns, so a rename never breaks history.
- `position` is a fractional string ordered `collate "C"`, written by our own small `positionBetween` (`packages/notes/src/position.ts`). No new dependency.
- A board's access reaches its cards: a card's role is the better of its note role and its board role, the board owner capped at edit.
- One shared default "Work" board per database (`boards.is_default`, unique). It is workspace edit, and cannot be archived or made private.
- The Notes home hides cards unless you search or ask for them.
- No label filter: notes have no tags. Filters are owner, client and blocked.
- "Owner" is `notes.assignee`. An agent that moves or closes a person's card into a done column lands it in Review (else the last open column) and is told. Unowned and agent-owned cards it can close.
- Needs you = open cards owned by you due today or earlier, plus cards waiting on a card you own. It shows as the Board tab badge and in Today, not a separate Home widget.
- Columns stay editable, but a column holding cards cannot be removed or flipped to done.
- Card comments are top-level threads with no text anchor.
- A card made from a note line goes on the viewer's default board. Its body names the source note, and the line gets a "Card: <column>" chip fixed at insert time. Backlinks show the live column.
- `notes.task` is served by the notes `records*` routes, and for Wren's team by the console's generic records.
- The List view row opens the record page, with an "Open the card" link to the note.
- Import updates column, owner and due on a link match, never the title. A bad owner, column or due warns and keeps the row. The CSV is built in the browser from the `taskExport` rows, with cells starting `= + - @` prefixed by `'`; "blocked by" is titles joined by "; ".
- A pasted file link becomes a labelled link. A Google file is renamed to its title (`driveTitle`: the account's metadata, else the shared page's title). Dropbox and OneDrive keep the kind label.
- Quick capture's "Card" is a switch in the Quick note dialog. ⌘K "card: <title>" and "New card" make one on the Work board.
- Not built: Ask Claude's "turn this note into cards" proposal in To-approve style. Agents add cards with `wren tasks add`; nothing writes unasked.

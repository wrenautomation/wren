## D4. Aerie Tasks (WA-1454): what changes from raycast-light D2

Raycast-light D2 stands for the List (the pane in the page, sized by `useRoom`; with Anton open and main under about 800 px, a task opens in the sheet). This adds, "to the tee" of v116:

- **Columns.** One per status, in this order: In Progress, In Testing, Needs Human Input (drawn "Needs you"), To Do, Backlog, Parked. Done hides under the default "Status is not Done" filter. The order lives in `apps/portal/web/src/modules/aerie/look.ts`.
- **Heads.** A small caps label and a count, in grey, in place of the `LANE` colours (William: "slightly less [colour] on the column title sections").
- **Card.** As the existing `Face`, plus who acts. Id, area chip, title, one line of detail, priority.
- **Who acts.** A word on the card and a field in the task's Details: "Agent", "With you", "You". "Waiting" shows instead when a dependency is not Done. No value: no word.

Where "who acts" comes from: the assignees cannot carry it. Of 76 open tasks, 60 have no assignee, and no task uses Aerie's `agent:` prefix (`aerie/src/store.ts`); `@claude` sits only on Done tasks.

| Option | How | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. A label | `acts:agent`, `acts:with-you`, `acts:you` in the task's labels; the agent sets it when it files a task | no schema change; Backlog.md keeps labels | labels also hold free words; a typo makes no word |
| B. A field | a new `acts` column in Aerie, mirrored to Backlog.md frontmatter | typed, one value | a schema change in Aerie and its mirror |
| C. From assignees | `agent:` only = Agent; a person and an agent = With you; a person only = You | no new data | blank on 60 of 76 open tasks |

**Recommend A.** Aerie reads the label into the row; the card shows the word. B later if the label drifts.

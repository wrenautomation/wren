# Offers, first class

2026-09-25. William is moving to recruiting firms with a free pilot. Before this, each offer
lived in email prose and lander YAML, typed by hand in both. Now an offer is registry data.
Every channel points at it, and every enrollment and deal carries its id.

## Shape

- `packages/offers`: `defineOffer` (checks one offer), `registry` (checks the set), `OFFERS`,
  `offerFor(id)`, `fits` / `invalidAnswers` (the application), `snapshot` (JSON for readers
  outside the repo). Pure data, no dependencies.
- `catalog/recruiting.ts`: `reactivation` (performance price, 3 slots, 30 days; live since
  2026-09-29), `recruiting-reactivation-pilot` (free; retired 2026-09-29) and
  `recruiting-candidate-reactivation` (paid extension, quoted).
  `catalog/operations.ts`: `ops-audit` (what the agencies and RIA arms pitch) and
  `ops-automation-build` ($10-15k upfront, $5-10k/mo; the top of every ladder).
- Niches: `NicheSpec.offers` maps each arm to the offer it pitches. `Niche.offers` maps
  sequence to offer. `defineNiche` refuses a sequence on a shared opener, an arm with no
  offer, an id the registry doesn't know, and an offer named for an arm nothing opens in.
- `enrollments.offer` (NOT NULL, indexed) is set by compose from `ComposeOptions.offer`.
  channel-email never imports the registry; it stores the id it was given. Migration 0011
  backfills `ops-audit`, because every enrollment before today pitched the audit.
- Lander: `lander/src/data/offers.json` is the snapshot, written by `pnpm offers:export`.
  `scripts/gates.sh lint` fails when it is stale, if `../lander` exists.

## Decisions

1. **An offer is terms, not copy.** The registry holds what must not drift between channels:
   promise, price, slots, days, what each side gives, guarantee, measures, the ladder and
   the application. Headlines and stories stay with each channel, where William edits them.
2. **An arm is one offer.** `sequences.ts` already defined an arm as "one offer, one hook".
   The niche names it, so attribution is exact. Two arms may pitch the same offer with
   different hooks. That's an A/B on the hook.
3. **Stamp, don't derive.** `enrollments.offer` is written at compose. If an arm's offer
   changes later, history stays true.
4. **Measures are declared per offer.** `measures` names the numbers that say an offer
   worked (job orders and fees for the pilot, hours for the build). Deals record values
   under those keys, so "was it worth it" is a query, not a memory.
5. **The application is data, and fit is one rule.** Questions and choices have stable
   snake_case ids (stored answers use them). Fit gates are `anyOf` on required choice
   questions. The rule stays that small because the lander evaluates it too (to decide
   whether to show the calendar), and wren re-checks with `fits` when it syncs.
6. **Snapshot, not a shared package.** The lander is Astro in its own repo with deploy-on-push.
   A committed JSON snapshot keeps its build offline and its diff reviewable. The gate
   catches drift. Moving the lander into this monorepo is the fix if drift ever bites.
7. **Scarcity is real or absent.** `slots` is the work one person can do well. The page says
   why it is three.
8. **Ladder is checked.** `next` must resolve, may not loop, and a live offer may not lead
   to a retired one. At most one live offer claims a page as its main offer.

9. **Emails quote terms, never type them.** `offerFacts(offer)` gives `offer.name`,
   `offer.days`, `offer.slots`, `offer.page`. `defineNiche` reads every `offer.*` key a
   template quotes and refuses the niche if the arm's offer doesn't set it. Compose merges
   the facts per offer (`Campaign.offerFacts`). Same rule as the lander's `{slots}`/`{days}`.
10. **A niche links a real page.** `niche.lander` (the sign-off's `{page}`) must be in
    `OFFER_PAGES`, the pages live offers claim, which are exactly the pages the site builds.
    `/` fills as the bare domain.
11. **A page is the service, then the offer** (2026-09-29). `/recruiting/lead-reactivation`,
    not `/recruiting`: each service family gets many offers and landers. Lowercase segments
    and hyphens only, no trailing slash. The bare family path 302s to its current page on
    the lander (`public/_redirects`) until the family has its own page.
12. **Performance is a price kind** (2026-09-29). `{kind: "performance", upfront, perUnit,
    unit, cap}`: a setup fee plus a fee per unit of outcome, capped (`cap` null for none).
    Whole dollars, `cap` at least one unit. Reactivation bills per meeting booked; the portal
    shows the running bill. Prices still never reach a page or an email (`offerFacts` has none).
13. **Ids never change.** Changing terms is a new offer: retire the old id, add the new one.
    Enrollments stamped with the old id keep meaning what was pitched.
14. **Reactivation's price, locked by William** (2026-10-01). Push A: $1,500 setup + $500 per
    meeting, setup and meetings $15,000 at most (`cap` 13,500 = 27 meetings), + $300/mo.
    Anchor B: $7,500 flat + $300/mo. Both 90 days, and support carries on past day 90
    until the 20th meeting, with no new setup fee. Once there are 20 meetings and day 90 has
    passed, it's done, and more is a new deal. Breakeven is about 12 meetings. The $5,000
    upfront figure is retired. The performance kind gained `monthly`, `flat` (B) and
    `until` (20). `wren delivery onboard reactivation --flat` issues B. The contract
    (version 2026-10-01.2) states the carry-on. Changed in place: no prices are pitched
    (D13 guards what was pitched), and a sold contract keeps its own terms.
    Later 10-01, his call: no meetings by day 90 means every fee back, on both options, if
    they gave at least 500 contacts that pass our checks, approved copy within 5 business days
    and didn't pull access or pause (`refundIfNone`, contract 2026-10-01.3). It makes the
    page's "If I don't get you results, you don't pay" true. The refund ends the work.

## Where to attack (ranked)

1. **Snapshot drift in CI.** The gate only runs where `../lander` exists. A wren commit from
   CI or another machine can change an offer without refreshing the lander. The fallback is
   the lander's own build, which validates the snapshot shape but can't know it is stale.
2. **Fit rule in two languages.** The lander's evaluator is a copy of `fits`. The rule is
   one line so they can't drift far, but a new rule kind needs both. Wren's check is the
   one that counts.
3. **Choice ids are forever.** Renaming a choice orphans stored answers. Retire the choice
   and add a new one; there is no check for this yet.
4. **`page` is one path.** An offer shown on two pages keeps one as main. If that gets
   awkward, `pages: string[]`.

# Public template gallery, 2026-10-09

Audit item (product-audit.md, "Public template gallery on the lander"): Zapier and Make list their
templates in public for search and sales proof. Ours were only inside the portal's Shop.

## What

- `pnpm templates:export <file> [--check]` (apps/worker `scripts/gallery-export.ts`) writes the
  code's client templates as JSON, like `offers:export`: id (dashed), name, blurb, icon, effects,
  and each step's part name and what it does there. Saved templates stay out: they are a client's
  or Wren's own wiring.
- The lander builds `/templates` (cards) and `/templates/<id>` (the steps, what it sends or spends,
  "Ask me to set it up" to the form) from `src/data/templates.json`.

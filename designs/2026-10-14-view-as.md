# View as, first class (2026-10-14)

William: "the toggle above Wren for demo, recruiting and test firm needs to go, it's buggy and
unnecessary. I need a first class feature as admin to view and test and use the portal through any
future client / user's view."

Today: the sidebar's top holds a workspace picker (`WorkspacePick` in packages/ui/src/shell.tsx)
listing Wren, the demo, recruiting and the test firm, plus a "View as client / Back to team view"
flip in the header (apps/portal/web/src/App.tsx). View as exists but is buried in Access → a
person, takes an email only, and is read only (`viewAs` in apps/portal/web/src/api.ts, the
Worker's 403 on writes, `viewAsOf` in packages/core/src/portal.ts).

## Decision

- **No workspace picker, no flip.** An operator's portal is Wren's. The demo, recruiting and the
  test firm are clients like any other, reached through View as.
- **View as** is a button in the operator's sidebar foot and a ⌘K command ("View as…"). One
  picker, searchable: a client, then who to be there:
  - a person on that client's team (default: its owner);
  - a role with nobody in it yet ("Someone new as Member"), custom roles included;
  - **a new client**: a blank sandbox client with the default install, made once, wiped on
    demand ("Start over"), to see and test what a client sees on day one. Synthetic only.
- **Look or Act.** Look (default) reads only, as today. Act does what that person could do,
  through their role and grants, with William recorded as the actor in the audit log
  ("William as amy@… at Acme"). Switching to Act on a real client asks once per tab: "Act as Amy
  at Acme? Every change is logged as you." Nothing skips its gates under Act: sends still wait in
  To approve, posts still need Approve, money still needs its own yes.
- **The strip.** While viewing as someone, a thin bar on top of every page: who, which client,
  Look / Act, Switch, Exit. Phones too. Scoped to the tab (sessionStorage), so other tabs stay
  William's.
- **The server decides.** Every call carries `viewAs: { client, email?, role?, act }`. The guard
  (`viewAsOf`) checks the real viewer is an operator, resolves the role and grants as that person
  (or that role), and refuses writes unless `act`. The Worker's blanket 403 becomes that check.
  Audit rows carry both `actor` and `as`.
- **Kept answers.** No device cache under View as (as today), so one person's view never leaks
  into another's.

## Build

1. Server: `viewAs` shape, guard, audit fields, the sandbox "new client" (create + reset handler,
   operator only), tests for Look refusing writes, Act writing under the role, a non-operator
   refused, role-only views, audit rows.
2. Client: remove `WorkspacePick` and the flip; the View as button, picker and ⌘K command; the
   strip; Access → person's "View as" opens the same picker preset.
3. Copy short and plain; never names a CLI command.

# Signals: site events, funnels per channel, session replay (2026-10-06)

William, 10-05: "Thoughts on posthog? I heard the price skyrockets at scale, so can we steal ur
features". On 10-06 he approved three of its features, built by us: site events tied to the
visitor, funnels per channel, and rrweb replay stored in S3. Flags, experiments, surveys and
heatmaps are out.

## Answer first

- **Events:** the lander keeps named events per view in D1 (`events`): CTA clicks, form
  start and submit, booking clicks, video plays and quarter marks, and any element marked
  `data-signal`. They carry the same visitor id and consent as `hits`.
- **Replay:** with consent only, the lander records the page with rrweb (every input masked).
  It sends a chunk every 10 seconds, and a Pages Function writes each chunk to S3
  (`site/replays/<view>/<seq>.json`, gzip) with a put-only key. A D1 `replays` row indexes
  each view. S3 expires the chunks after 90 days.
- **Funnels:** `site_days` gains `calls` (real cal.com bookings) and `paid` (engagements by
  their first paid invoice), both under first-touch channel and campaign. Marketing → Funnel
  shows visitors → forms → calls → paid per channel, over 30 days, 90 days and all time.
- **Watching:** Marketing → Sessions lists recent replays, read live from the lander and not
  stored in wren (the 09-29 rule about visitors still holds). A row says what brought the
  visitor and whether they applied. Opening one plays it; the worker signs a 10-minute GET
  per chunk.

## Lander

- **`events`:** id, ts, view, visitor, page, name, props (JSON, 1 KB max). Names are
  `[a-z][a-z0-9_.]{0,39}`, at most 50 per post. `POST /api/events` takes a batch, and the
  page flushes it when hidden.
- **What fires, all from `src/scripts/hit.ts` with delegated listeners:**
  - `cta` (label and target);
  - `form.start` and `form.submit`;
  - `book.click`;
  - `video.play` and `video.progress` (25, 50, 75, 100);
  - `[data-signal="<name>"]` clicks.
- **`replays`:** view (unique), visitor, page, started, last, chunks, bytes, w, country, and
  `capped`.
- **`POST /api/replay?view=&seq=`:**
  - Needs a consenting visitor, and caps a chunk at 1 MB.
  - Gzips the chunk and PUTs it to S3 with `Content-Encoding: gzip`, then upserts the row.
  - Stops a view at `REPLAY_MAX_BYTES` (5 MB).
- **Recorder** (`src/scripts/replay.ts`): loaded only when `/api/hit` answers
  `replay: true`. That means consent is yes, the keys are set, and the visit falls in the
  `REPLAY_SAMPLE` share (default 1).
  - Every input is masked, and `[data-private]` is blocked.
  - No canvas, no fonts; images are recorded as links.
  - The motion stage's style churn is held down with `ignoreCSSAttributes` or blocked
    selectors, tuned on a real visit.
- **Export:** `/api/export?table=events|replays` pages the new tables by id.
- **Privacy page:** one line about replay: masked inputs, 90 days, only with the cookie yes.

## AWS

- **Bucket:** the existing private files bucket, under prefix `site/replays/`. Encryption,
  the public block and CORS are already set.
- **New:**
  - IAM user `lander-replays`, allowed only `s3:PutObject` on `site/replays/*`, with one
    access key in Pages secrets (`REPLAY_KEY_ID`, `REPLAY_SECRET`, plus `REPLAY_BUCKET` and
    `REPLAY_REGION`). Terraform also writes all four to SSM `/wren/prod/lander-replays`, so
    they are never read from `tofu output`. A leak can only upload junk, and the junk expires.
  - A lifecycle rule that expires `site/replays/` after 90 days.
  - CORS: allow `GET` from the app (the player fetches the chunks).
  - The worker gets `s3:GetObject` on `site/replays/*`.
- **Apply:** `tofu apply -target` on these resources only (a full plan shows unrelated drift).

## wren

- **Migration:** `site_days.calls` and `site_days.paid` (0104).
- **The rollup** (`rollupSite`, the pass that already reads the export):
  - `call_bookings` that weren't cancelled count under a channel, dated by `booked_at`:
    - a booking with a link code counts as email (`touchChannel({ r })`);
    - otherwise the attendee email is matched to an application's first touch;
    - otherwise it goes under `other`.
  - Engagements with `source_channel` count as `paid` on their first paid invoice's day.
- **Records:**
  - `marketing.funnel`: one row per (window, channel), with visitors, forms, calls, paid
    and the step rates. Views 30d, 90d and all.
  - `marketing.session`: rows read live from the export (replays, plus the visitor's
    applications and first touch). Bound only when the export token and files bucket are set.
- **Player:** the session record's `load` returns signed chunk URLs. The portal plays them
  with `rrweb-player` in a framed `/replay` page (69 KB gzip), so only that page's CSP allows
  inline styles and the lander's images and fonts.

## Cost

- **Today:** $0. S3 holds about 200 KB per session.
- **At 50k sessions a month:**
  - about 10 GB in S3 ($0.23);
  - 300k PUTs ($1.50);
  - Cloudflare requests: past 100k a day, Workers Paid at $5 a month.

  PostHog charges about $173 a month for that many replays.

# Touches: every social touch, per person (2026-10-07)

William, 2026-10-07 (relayed): every social touch we make must be tracked per person across
LinkedIn, X, IG and Reddit: follows, connects, comments on their posts, replies to their comments,
DMs, and their response. Outreach should cite earlier touches next to the dossier ("we've talked
before, and I know this about you").

Other side of the seam: autobrowse `sites` routes (LinkedIn `/connections`, `/in/{vanity}/withdraw`,

## Answer

One model in core: `social_handles` (one person on one platform) and `touches` (one touch, ours or
theirs, with their response). Every send and read point writes a touch as it writes its own row.
The DM drafter, compose and the pre-call brief read them back. Marketing → People shows a Touches
tab on every person. `wren touches <who>` prints the timeline.

## Shape

`social_handles`: `platform`, `handle` (normalized), `url`, `name`, `person_id`, `lead_id`,
`linked_by`. Unique on (platform, handle).

`touches`: `handle_id`, `kind` (follow, connect, comment, reply, dm, like, mention), `direction`
(ours, theirs), `account` (ours), `url` (the post or thread), `text`, `at`, `response` (accepted,
replied, liked, ignored), `response_at`, `answers` (theirs → the ours touch it answers),
`external_id` (the platform's id), `source`, `ref` (unique, from the source row).

Refs: `rm:<id>` reach message, `c:<id>` their comment, `ca:<id>` our answer to it, `rt:<id>` our
Reddit thread comment, `rp:<id>` our comment on someone's post (LinkedIn, X, Instagram), with
`rp:<id>:like` and `rp:<id>:follow` for the like and follow that went with it, `sa:<id>` social activity. A live
writer and the backfill write the same ref, so neither keeps a touch twice.

Handles (`normalizeHandle`, `@wren/core/touches`):

| Platform | Key | From |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | vanity, lowercased and decoded | `/in/<vanity>` on any subdomain, bare vanity |
| LinkedIn | `urn:li:person:<id>` | a URN, as comments on our posts name authors |
| LinkedIn | `company:<slug>` | `/company/<slug>`, a company page's post |
| X | handle, lowercased | `@name`, `x.com/name`, `twitter.com/name` |
| X | `id:<n>` | the numeric author id X comments carry |
| Instagram | handle, lowercased | `@name`, `instagram.com/name` |
| Reddit | username, lowercased | `u/name`, `/user/name`, bare |
| any | `name:<words>` | a display name where a handle should be: kept, linked by hand |

Resolver (`resolveHandle`): a handle links to a person by, in order, its reach contact's
`person_id`, `people.linkedin_url` (LinkedIn vanity), then `contact_points` (LinkedIn, X,
Instagram profile URLs). The lead is the person's lead (verified first), else a lead whose
`social_url` is the same handle. No match: the handle row stands as the person until one links;
`relinkHandles` (run by the backfill) tries again, and `wren touches link` links by hand.

Writers:

| Point | Touch |
|---|---|
| `recordSent` (tick) | ours `connect` or `dm` |
| `receive` (DM inbox) | theirs `dm`; ours DMs before it read `replied` |
| `markAccepted`, `applyWithdraw` | the invite reads `accepted`, or `ignored` once withdrawn or gone |
| `keepComments` (reach inbox), `keepPostComments` (SocialWatch) | theirs `comment`, `reply` or `mention`; a reply whose parent is ours marks it `replied` |
| `markAnswered` | ours `reply` |
| `markCommented` (Reddit thread) | ours `comment` on the OP, or `reply` to the comment that asked |
| `markPostCommented` (LinkedIn, X, Instagram) | ours `comment` |
| `markPostActed` (after his Comment, when the settings ask) | ours `like` on the post, ours `follow` of its author |
| `keepActivity` | theirs `follow` (follow, subscribe), `like` (reaction), `mention` |

A touch write never fails its send: `keepTouch` logs and moves on, and the backfill writes it again.

Readers:

- DM drafter (`dmContext`): "Earlier touches with them, newest first" between the facts and the
  thread, every platform, without the thread's own DMs and invite. Our own earlier comments count
  as his words for the guard.
- Compose (`factsFor`): `touch.line` ("your comment on our LinkedIn post"), `touch.platform`,
  `touch.when`, `touch.days`, `touch.count`, `touch.context`. A template cites one in a `(( ))`
  group, which drops when there is none; a `<<prompt>>` slot reads `{touch.context}`. Only a
  mutual touch, or a public comment of ours, is cited.
- Pre-call brief: "Earlier touches" part, and `Earlier touch on <site>` lines in what the model
  reads for questions.
- `wren dossier`: a `touch` fact per touch on each person.
- Marketing → People: Touches tab (`person_touch_lines`), Touches and Last touch columns, a
  Touched view, and a row for every handle a touch met that People didn't list.

"No answer" is read, not kept: an ours comment, reply, DM or invite with no response after 14 days.

## In development

- Their like on our comment or post (`liked`): no platform read returns it per person.
- Replies to our LinkedIn comment on their post: the comment route returns no id, and nothing
  reads that thread back.
- Follows and likes outside a reach post (from a People row): only reach posts send them.
- Reddit DM contacts rarely carry a person: link by hand.

## Decision log

- 2026-10-10: Like only and Follow only on a post in To approve (`ReachDesk.actOnPost`, `wren
  reach posts like|follow <id>`): the click is the yes whatever the settings say, the post stays
  to comment on, and each is the same `rp:<id>:like|follow` touch.
- 2026-10-09 (gaps item 4): `linkedin_posts` became `reach_posts` (migration 0209) for LinkedIn,
  X and Instagram. His Comment sends the like and follow the platform's settings ask for
  (`like` on, `follow` off), as the reading account, each a touch; a failed one never fails the
  comment. X comment authors now come back as usernames (autobrowse asks for `includes.users`;
  the page leg already said `author_username`). LinkedIn's API names a commenter only by an
  app-scoped URN no read resolves, so `linkCommentAuthors` matches it to the comment notice
  ("Jane commented on your post"): by the comment's id in the notice link, else the only notice
  on that post within two days. Two people on one post the same day stay URNs.

- 2026-10-07: new tables, not an extension. `reach_messages` holds DMs and invites only and
  needs a reach contact per row; comments, threads, posts and activity live in five other tables.
  Under 80% there.
- 2026-10-07: core owns the model, so channel-email (compose, brief) reads it without importing
  outreach. Outreach and content own their writers; the backfill sits in content, which sees both.
- 2026-10-07: a touch is keyed by its source row, not hashed from its content, so a re-read or a
  backfill never doubles it.
- 2026-10-07: a handle we can't link is kept as its own person. Nothing is dropped for want of a
  key.
- 2026-10-07: "ignored" is kept only when known (an invite withdrawn or gone); otherwise read at
  14 days, so a late answer still counts.
- 2026-10-07: the backfill was not run on prod. Run `wren touches backfill --dry-run`, then
  `wren touches backfill`.

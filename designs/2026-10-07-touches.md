William, 2026-10-07 (relayed): every social touch we make must be tracked per person across
LinkedIn, X, IG and Reddit: follows, connects, comments on their posts, replies to their comments,
DMs, and their response. Outreach should cite earlier touches next to the dossier ("we've talked

## Audit (read only, prod counts 2026-10-07)

and only DM contacts carried a person. Nothing joined them per person, and nothing read them into
outreach except the DM drafter (two comments on our posts).

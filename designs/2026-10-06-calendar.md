# Calendar: our own booking, replacing Cal.com

2026-10-06. William: "Custom calendar system to replace calendly / cal". Waiting on his yes.

## Why

- Cal.com is the last outside tool between a lead and a call. Its webhook, metadata hacks
  (utm_* dropped on v2) and branding all go away.
- Calendars are part of the GHL full stack (designs/2026-10-05-end-goal.md). Clients need their
  own booking pages, and that's ours to sell.
- The booking lands in our own tables, so booked counts, follow-up stops and attribution read it
  directly, with no forwarding.

## What it is

- **Availability:** rules per calendar: weekly hours, time zone, notice, buffer, length and daily
  cap. Busy times come from Google Calendar free/busy (google@wren's calendar for Wren; a client
  connects theirs).
- **Booking page:** `wrenautomation.com/book/<offer>` keeps its URL and stops redirecting. It
  shows slots in the visitor's time zone and asks for name and email. Booking calls the wren
  API.
- **Book:** a Postgres row with a unique slot per calendar, so two people can't take one slot.
  The Google event comes from `events.insert` with a Meet link and `sendUpdates=all`, so Google
  sends the invite. Then the same `CallBookings` path the webhook feeds today: stop follow-ups,
  count the booking, record the source.
- **Change:** reschedule and cancel links in the confirmation, signed, one per booking.
- **Reminders:** 24 h and 1 h before, by email and, when we have the number and consent, by text.
  Both through the existing channels.
- **Portal:** Calendar app with upcoming calls, past calls and no-shows, plus availability in the
  Shop part's settings.

## Cost

$0. Google Calendar API free tier; pages on the lander and wren API on Lambda.

## Build order (after his yes)

1. Tables, availability, slots, book and cancel in wren; Google event with Meet. Tests on
   synthetic calendars.
2. Lander `/book/<offer>` page against it, behind a flag; Cal.com stays the default.
3. Reminders, reschedule, portal Calendar app, Shop part.
4. Switch the lander to it, then retire the Cal.com webhook. Per-client calendars after that.

## Open

- Round robin and team calendars: later, when a client has more than one closer.
- Paid bookings: not planned.

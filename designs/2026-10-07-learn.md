# Learn: reels, videos and feeds in one place (2026-10-07)

William, 2026-10-07: "first class reel history / ingestion for SOP generation / insights,
connected to a global content ingestion dashboard that has notification systems for all your
favorite content sources, youtubers, blogs, etc. so you can ingest information and upskill
quickly."

## Answer first

- **A Learn app**, Wren's workspace only. Pages: Overview, Items, Saved, Sources, Search, SOPs.
  The radar's feeds and items move here from the Inbox. Inbox keeps mail.
- **Share in from the phone.** An iOS Shortcut opens `/learn/add?url=<link>` in Safari with his
  portal session. The page saves the link and says so. Same for the "Save a link" box and
  `wren learn add <url>`.
- **Every item keeps its transcript** (speech plus what's on screen), a summary, a 0 to 10 score
  against the pushed SOPs, what it would change, and its source. All of it in the database.
- **Ingest on the spine.** A save or a new feed item enters the `learn` workflow: read, then
  score. Articles are read on the worker. Videos and reels need yt-dlp, a home IP and the Gemini
  key fleet, so they wait for the reader on the Mac (`wren learn read`).
- **To SOP.** "Add to SOP" on any item asks for that SOP's next source. The Mac writes it into
  `../sops/<name>/sources/` from the stored transcript and extracts its points. Build and push
  stay William's.
- **Tell me**, per source: every item, score 8 and up, or digest only. Alerts batch on the
  Monitor's 15 minute pass, like urgent flags. One digest at 09:00 lists the rest.
- **Search** across every transcript with Postgres full text.

## Where it lives

- `packages/learn` (`@wren/learn`): the `learn` schema, feeds (moved from `@wren/watch`), readers,
  scoring, alerts, search, SOP links, `LearnConsole`, records, components.
- The Monitor (`@wren/watch`) keeps mail. Its 15 minute loop still pulls the feeds and now sends
  the alerts and the digest, so Learn needs no loop of its own.
- Web: `apps/portal/web/src/modules/learn/`.

Why an app and not the Inbox: Inbox is what waits on a reply, already 12 tabs. Learning is a
different job with five pages of its own. One home: the Inbox drops Worth reading and Feeds.

## Data (`learn` schema, main only)

| Table | Holds |
|---|---|
| `sources` | a followed feed: url, the page it came from, name, kind (youtube, podcast, blog, forum, releases), `tell` (every, top, digest), last read, last error, stopped |
| `items` | url (unique, cleaned of tracking params), kind (article, video, reel, episode), title, creator, the feed or page `text`, the reader's `transcript`, its SOP source file name, read state, score, verdict, summary, `changes` (SOP names), `why`, saved at, by and via, told at, done at, and a generated `search` tsvector with a GIN index |
| `sop_sources` | an item asked into an SOP: sop, file, state (asked, added, failed), error, who, when |

`watch.feeds` and `watch.items` are dropped: prod has 0 rows in each.

## Pipeline

```
save (portal, Shortcut, CLI) ─┐
                              ├─▶ read ──▶ score ──▶ show | hold | drop
Monitor pass: new feed items ─┘     │
                                    └─ video or reel: waits for the Mac reader
```

- `learn.read` (worker): an article keeps its feed text when it has enough, else the page is
  fetched and stripped to text. A video, reel or podcast episode is marked "waits for the Mac".
- `wren learn read` (Mac, add `--every 10` to keep going): reads each waiting video with the
  `sop add` readers (`youtubeSource`: captions plus Gemini on screen; `videoSource`: a 540p
  download, Gemini for speech and screen), stores the markdown as the transcript, then scores it
  with the same scorer.
- `learn.score`: the radar's scorer, now on the transcript. 7 and up shows, 4 to 6 holds.
  Model: `WREN_WATCH_LLM` (Cohere on prod). The CLI defaults to the gateway, $0.
- A saved item never alerts: he saved it.

## Share from the iPhone

iOS Safari can't be a share target, so a Shortcut opens the portal:

1. Shortcuts, new shortcut, name it "Save to Wren".
2. Its info: Show in Share Sheet on; receive URLs and Text.
3. Add "Get URLs from Input" (Shortcut Input).
4. Add "URL Encode" on the URLs.
5. Add "Open URLs": `https://app.wrenautomation.com/learn/add?url=` then the encoded text.
6. From Instagram, TikTok, X or YouTube: Share, Save to Wren. Safari opens; the page says Saved.

No session: the page signs in and comes back to the same address. Saving the same link twice
keeps one item. Nothing here logs into his Instagram or TikTok.

## Sources

- Follow takes any address. A feed is followed as is. A page (a YouTube channel, a Substack, a
  blog, a podcast site) is read for its `<link rel="alternate">` feed.
- Following reads the back catalog as seen: it scores what comes next.
- Instagram, TikTok and X creators: in development. Public profile reads need a home IP (yt-dlp
  on the Mac) or Instagram's business discovery through autobrowse. Save their posts one at a
  time until then.
- Podcast episodes keep their show notes; audio transcripts are in development (Gemini's inline
  cap is about 15 minutes of audio).

## To SOP

- "Add to SOP" (portal) or `wren learn to-sop <item> <sop>` writes a `sop_sources` row.
- On the Mac, `wren learn read` (or `to-sop` itself) writes `sources/<file>.md` from the stored
  transcript with the same front matter `sop add` writes, then runs `sop extract` for that one
  source. The row turns added, with the file name.
- The item's page lists its SOPs. The SOPs page lists each SOP folder with its sources, which
  items they came from, the last build and whether it's pushed. It reads `../sops` where the
  folder exists (the CLI, the local preview). On the worker it reads only the database: pushed
  SOPs and the links, with folders marked in development.
- Later, SOPs in the database: a `sops` table (name, notes, SOP.md versions) and `sop_sources`
  holding the source text, with the Mac's folder as a checkout written by `wren sop pull`.
  Build stays on Claude Code on the Mac.

## Notifications

- Per source, "Tell me": every item, score 8 and up (default), or digest only.
- The Monitor's pass sends the waiting alerts in one message, `action` level, on the main
  notifier. At 09:00 send time, one digest lists everything scored since the last one that
  wasn't told, best first. A day sends at most one digest.
- No new Discord lane: SSM `/wren/prod/env` is nearly full. Split it, then add
  `WREN_DISCORD_LEARN_WEBHOOK_URL`.
- Inbox stays inbound only. Nothing here asks for an approval.

## Search

- `items.search`: title (A), summary (B), transcript or text (C), English config. `LearnConsole`
  `search` runs `websearch_to_tsquery`, ranks, and returns a highlighted snippet.
- Embeddings: in development. Gemini embeddings are free; pgvector isn't on the box yet.

## CLI

`wren learn add <url> [--sop <name>]`, `follow <url> [--tell every|top|digest]`,
`list [--saved] [--waiting]`, `search <words>`, `read [--every <min>]`, `to-sop <item> <sop>`.

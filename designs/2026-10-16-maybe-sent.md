# Maybe sent

2026-10-16. A send whose outcome is unknown is checked on the site, recorded once, or parked for a person. It never pauses Restate and never sends twice.

## What happened

On 2026-10-15 two Reddit thread comments (`ReachDesk/commentThread`, called by `RedditReads/wren/loop`) posted, but the send step failed with a 502: `"save the comment" already went through on an earlier try of this step; check it before running again`. `restateSites` journals each desk write, so a retry replayed the same error. `commentThread` made only 4xx terminal, so Restate retried, then paused both calls. The `reddit_threads` rows stayed `queued` with no `answer_ref`, so a later schedule pass could have posted them again. A fresh resend would have read the thread, found our own handle in it, and refused without recording anything.

## Decisions

- **One ledger, `send_attempts`** (`packages/outreach/src/schema.ts`, migration `0280_send_attempts`). One row per item that must land at most once, keyed by its typed id: `thread:<id>`, `comment:<id>`, `onpost:<id>`, `dm:<reach message id>`, `private:<comment id>` (a DM from a comment on our own post), `sdm:<reach message id>` (a client's DM answer through its connected account, in the client's own database). A claim marks it `sending` before any call. A second invocation meanwhile is refused (409). A claim on a `sending` row older than 10 minutes takes it over and knows an earlier try may have left. Settled `sent` (with the real ref and `settled_by`: `sent`, `found` or the person) or `unsure`. A refusal, or a send that never left, deletes the row so the item sends as if never tried.
- **One reconcile, `sendOnce`** (`packages/core/src/maybe-sent.ts`). `sendFate` reads autobrowse's words before the status: "may have gone through" or "already went through" is maybe; a refusal, or any 4xx but a 408, left nothing; "not sent" or a busy browser is not sent. On maybe it waits 30 s (`ctx.sleep`), then looks for our words by the sending account. Found: recorded with its ref and time. Absent (a listing that reaches back past the send): one more send. Unclear, or absent a second time: `unsure`. It also looks before the first send when the claim says an earlier try may have left, and an unreadable look then is `unsure` with no send.
- **On Restate, `sendAtMostOnce`** (`packages/outreach/src/restate/maybe-sent.ts`). Claim and settle are steps; the send and the look are the caller's journaled site calls. Unsure throws `SendUnsure`, a `TerminalError` (409), so nothing retries or pauses. A scheduled slot leaves the schedule. `sendError` makes a refusal or a journaled site error terminal too.
- **Before sending, our own words.** The look runs first whenever the item was tried. The existing "someone of ours already wrote here" guard (`threadAuthors`, `checkThread`) runs only after the look says our words are not there, so a resend after a crash records the comment instead of refusing.
- **A person settles `unsure`** in To approve (view "May have gone out"): Open on site, Mark posted, Send again. Both go to `ReachDesk/settleSend {item, outcome}` (effect sends). Mark posted records it the way a send does (`markCommented`, `markAnswered`, `markPostCommented`, or the sender's `recordSent` with its sequence step) and schedules the proof checks. Send again calls the item's own handler with `again: true`, which looks first. A DM goes back in the sender's queue. A private reply records through `privateReplied`; Send again calls `ReachDesk/dmComment` with the ledger's words.
- **A client settles its own** in its To approve (`marketing.asked_reply` lists its `unsure` rows). Mark posted and Send again go to `MarketingConsole/sendSettle`, its approver only, then `SocialInbox/settle` on its database: `sdm:` records through `sentDm`, `comment:` through `markAnswered`; Send again calls `SocialInbox/send` or `answer` with `again: true`. `planDm` reuses a row still `sending` or `unknown` with the same words, so a second click is the same item; the same words sent under 10 minutes ago are refused.
- **Our own posts too (`draft:<id>`, wa-43x).** On 2026-10-16 a TikTok publish came back "already went through … may have gone through". `ContentScheduler` marked the draft `failed`, but the video was live. A failed draft can be approved again and post twice, and its numbers are never read. Now `ContentScheduler` sends any maybe (`sendFate`) to a look, never to `failed`. It waits 30 s, then calls `Content/findPost`: our channel's `list` (20 newest) as the account it went out as, read by `findPost`/`seenPost` (`core/maybe-sent.ts`). A match is a row from the start of the send on (less the clock slack) whose preview starts with our words: the text as sent, the draft's text, or its title (`lookWords`; a thread's first post). Found: `markPublished` with the platform's id, url and time, the first comment, the posted hook. Absent (the listing's oldest row is older than the send): one more publish, only once. Else the draft stays `publishing`, with "May have gone out: …" as its error, and parks `unsure` in To approve. No row is claimed at the start, because `publishing` already keeps every other pass off it.
- **A channel whose listing is late never proves absent.** `ContentChannel.listsLate(media)` names why. TikTok lists a video only once processed and public. The YouTube uploads list lags, and has no community posts. A Facebook or Instagram video lists only once processed. On those, not found is `unsure`, never a second post.
- **A person settles a post** with the same Mark posted / Send again. `ReachDesk/settleSend` (Wren's) and `SocialInbox/settle` (a client's) send `draft:` to `ContentDesk/<key>/settlePost` (service calls only). It looks first: found is published with the platform's id, whatever he chose. Else Mark posted marks it published on his word, with no id, so its numbers are not read. Send again puts it back to `approved` and wakes the scheduler.
- **Schedulers leave open items alone.** `schedulePosts` and `scheduleThreads` skip any item with a `sending` or `unsure` row. Engage hides them; To approve shows them in place of the queued row.

## Paths

| Send | Look | On unknown |
|---|---|---|
| Reddit thread comment (`commentThread`) | `findSent`: `/user/{self}/comments`, parent and words | found, retry once, or unsure |
| Reddit reply to a comment (`answerComment`, reach path) | `findSent` on the comment | same |
| Reddit DM (sender `tick`) | `findSent`: `/message/sent`, recipient and words | found or retry; unsure leaves the message `unknown` and parks it |
| LinkedIn connect (sender) | `/in/{vanity}/relationship`: pending or connected | found, absent, or unclear |
| LinkedIn DM (sender) | `/messaging` preview, our words | found only; else unsure |
| Answer on our own post (`answerComment`, content path) | `Content.comments`, rows marked mine; X: `Content.findReply` | found only; else unsure |
| Comment on someone's post, LinkedIn (`commentPost`) | `Content.comments`, mine | found only; else unsure |
| Comment on someone's post, Instagram (`commentPost`, a site call) | `igCommentLook`: autobrowse `instagram GET /web/p/{shortcode}/comments` as the sending account, rows marked `mine` (the nav's signed-in user), our words | found, retry once (the whole list read and the viewer known), or unsure (Instagram ranks comments, so a cut list can't tell) |
| Reply on someone's post, X (`commentPost`) | `Content.findReply`: search `from:me to:<handle>`, else `from:me conversation_id:<post>`, our words | found only (search lags a fresh post); else unsure |
| Client's comment answer (`SocialInbox/answer`, `comment:`) | `contentLook` on the client's own channels, as for ours | found only; else unsure |
| Client's DM answer (`SocialInbox/send`, `sdm:`) | `readDms` as the connected account: their conversation, our words (`seenInDms`) | found, retry once (the read's oldest message is older than the send), or unsure; unsure leaves the message `unknown` |
| Our own post (`ContentScheduler`, `draft:`): X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Threads | `Content/findPost`: the channel's `list`, our words from the send on (`seenPost`) | found, publish once more (the list reaches past the send), or unsure |
| Our own post: TikTok (`/v2/video/list/`), YouTube (uploads), Facebook Page video, Instagram video | the same | found or unsure (`listsLate`) |
| Our own post: Facebook Page text or photo, Instagram photo (Graph `/{id}/media`, or `graph.instagram.com /me` then `/{user_id}/media` on the web login) | the same | found, publish once more, or unsure |
| Meta private reply (`dmComment`, `private:`) | `Content.findPrivateReply`: autobrowse `meta GET /{pageId}/conversations` on the Page token (Messenger, or `platform=instagram`), the author's conversation by handle or name, our words since the send (`seenPrivateReply`, `core/content/page-inbox.ts`) | found, retry once (their whole conversation read, or no conversation with them in an inbox read past the send), or unsure; Meta takes one private reply per comment, so a wrong retry is refused |

No read can confirm these, so an unknown outcome is `unsure` and waits on a person:

- A Meta private reply when the read fails (an Instagram inbox needs `instagram_manage_messages` on the Page token), the comment row has no author, or their conversation is cut before the send.
- An Instagram comment on a post whose ranked list the read did not reach to its end, or whose page did not show the signed-in user.
- Our own post on a channel with no `list` (a client's channel without one): the look is unclear.
- A connect anywhere but LinkedIn: no other platform sends one (Reddit has none).

Not on the ledger: follows and likes (`postActs`, `actOnPost`) are idempotent on the site, so a second send changes nothing. Keyword DMs (`packages/content/src/restate/keyword-dm.ts`) still call `Content.privateReply` straight; they move to `private:` with the next change there.

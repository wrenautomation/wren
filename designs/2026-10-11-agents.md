# Agents page

2026-10-11. From William's temp_notes: colton.ai.dean's builds (Learn items 13 to 19) look better than ours. How do we get there with the component system? Should we add more visual components (React Flow, shadcn)?

## What his builds show

- A "war room": every agent as a card, grouped by department, each with its numbers and state.
- A chief of staff: what needs you today, and a chat with it.
- "The brains": a graph of what each agent reads.
- A funnel (contacts → dialed → connected → qualified → meetings → revenue) with ROI.
- Niche widgets: a storm map, roof measuring, a call simulator.

## What we already had

- React Flow (`@xyflow/react`), Recharts and shadcn parts live in `@wren/ui`. No new library is needed.
- The brains: the workflow canvas (`/workflows/canvas`) draws every part, its wires, live counts and real events as dots. The component Map (`/marketplace/map`) draws what builds on what.
- Funnels: the sites funnel graph and the canvas's wire rates.
- The chief of staff: To approve, Inbox, and Ask (`/ask/questions`).
- Customizing: Overview tiles and rail pins, kept per viewer (`customize.tsx`).

The gap was the war room: nothing showed the parts as agents doing work. The loops page is a table of service names.

## Decision

Built `/loops/agents`:

- Every built part that runs for Wren (`for: "wren"` or `wrenSettings`) is a card (`AppCard`).
- Cards are grouped by department, which is the part's stage, in the stages' order.
- Each card shows:
  - its state from its loops: running, failing, off, or "when called" for a part with no loop;
  - its last pass;
  - each counted out port over 30 days and today.
- The head shows how many are running, failing and off, plus everything made today.
- A failing card opens Loops → Failing. Any other card opens its part in the catalog.
- Server side, `ConsolePortal/agents` returns the lines (team only). States and numbers come from the existing `loops` and `recordsStats` handlers.
- The model is in `apps/portal/web/src/modules/wren/agent-model.ts`. It is pure and tested.

A new part shows up here on its own once it's defined. Nothing is drawn per agent by hand. That's how the component system gets his look.

## Not now

- Niche widgets such as maps and roof measuring. Each is a client part's own page, built when a client in that niche needs one. A map would use MapLibre, free tiles.
- Hours saved per agent. That needs a minutes-per-unit guess on each port, which is William's number to set.

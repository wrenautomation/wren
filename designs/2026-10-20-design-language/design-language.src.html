02Leaf and sweep

From the mark's feather. Two opposite corners round, two stay tight. Both round tips get a lavender sweep. An open band adds a fainter second line inside each tip, so the live band stands out. Checks and avatars stay plain.

Rules
  1. Full leaf on panels, bands, cards, chips, nav rows and bars. Buttons get a soft hint only: 6 / 10, so they read as buttons first.
  2. The sweep goes on panels and bands only. Faint when closed, full on hover or open.
  3. The second, inner sweep shows only on an open band.
  4. Checkboxes, avatars and map markers keep plain round shapes. They carry meaning; the motif does not.

04Metro map

The business map draws each offer as a line people ride. Each visual channel carries one fact. No channel carries two.

ColourWhich offerOne colour per offer, for its whole life. Grey means no offer yet: outreach.
Line weightThe job of the offerThin grey is outreach. Medium is front end. Thick is core. Weight never shows volume.
Station iconWhat the person givesEye is attention. Clock is time. $ is money. Exit icon: they leave. Plus: planned.
TrackWho moves them onThe system (plain track) or a person (a badge with the owner and the promised time).
ColumnsWhich part of the businessMarketing, Sales, Delivery, Money. Every map reads left to right in that order.

05Zoom levels

Zoom out and a run of stations folds into one capsule. Reddit to a booked call is six stations up close. Zoomed out, it is a Reddit capsule, the hub and a Sales capsule. Zoom is a view. It never changes colour or weight, and it hides stations, never lines. Pinch, or use the switch. Click a capsule to open it.

PartsOne capsule per line in each column. The whole business on one screen.
GroupsThe default. Runs you name fold: "Reddit", "Onboarding". Work stations stay open.
StationsEvery station, every badge.
  • The capsule is the one new mark. It runs from the first to the last station it holds, in the line's colour and weight.
  • The number inside is the people in the run now. The name sits above, like a station name.
  • Tracks, transfers and exits attach to the capsule's edge. The audit upsell still shows when both ends fold.
  • An amber ring means a person track inside is late. Here, Reddit comments promise 4 h and take a day.
  • The hub and exits never fold. One level of groups only: no group inside a group.

07The map in the app

The map is the home page. Today sits under it. Every person track becomes a task with an owner and a promise, so the day's list comes from the map, not from a separate to-do app. Late tasks go first. Hover a task to light its line.

The sidebar has five items: Map, Inbox, People, Money, Admin. Every other app opens from the station or line that uses it: the YouTube line opens posting, a DM station opens Outreach. Admin → Systems lists every app in two groups, on your shape (it names the stations) and not on the shape (debug and internal). Pin an app to keep it one click away. ⌘K finds any app, page or station.

08Offers, plans and bundles

The map shows the route. The sheet holds the deal. A sample business with three offers, two packages per offer, five payment plans, one bundle and one credit. Click any station, track, badge or transfer. In the app this sheet opens beside the map.

PackageSame route, different scopeStandard and Plus share one line. If a package changes the delivery steps, it is a new offer.
Payment planWhen cash comes in, not where people goA chip on the money station: "3 plans". A plan with more than one payment adds a Failed payment exit.
BundleOne purchase, several linesA stacked $ station. Its tracks fork into each line it starts, in that line's colour.
CreditLives on the transfer"$500 credit" sits on the upsell label. It belongs to the move, not to either offer.
Add-on and package changeSheet onlyA checkout add-on or a move from Standard to Plus is history on the person. No map mark.

Inside an offer: delivery steps and loops

An offer line holds its own delivery. Steps that happen once run in a straight line. Steps that repeat every month form a ring, and the monthly bill sits on the ring. A client who leaves can come back on a grey rewarm line. Dots show the way people move. The map opens zoomed out, at Offer level.

Once stepsA straight run after the saleKickoff, logins, build week. Each step says what the client gives and who owns it.
LoopA ring on the offer lineEach lap is one cycle. The bill is a station on the ring. The centre chip gives the cycle and how many clients are in it.
Leaving a loopAn exit from the station where the client decidesThe review call. Churn and failed payment are red.
RewarmA grey line from a failure back to the hubStations are waits and touches. It has a cap: two rewarms, then the person rests for good.
ZoomOffer, Groups, StationsGroups: once steps fold into a capsule, the loop into a ring. Offer: the whole line is one badge. Hub and red exits never fold. Click a folded mark to open it; click its fold tab to close it.

Pricing model

A price is stored once, on the offer. Stations, bundles and proposals point to it. A purchase records the package and the plan, and the plan schedule writes the money events.

09Logic

The map shows where people go. The sheet holds the logic. Each kind of logic event has its own mark, so a glance tells an if from a race, a split or a test. One sample protocol holds every mark. It opens folded: click a group to open it, click its tab to fold it. Send holds a second fold inside it.

11Target state: the shape model

The map can only draw what the model stores. Today an offer is a node on the graph. In the target, an offer is a line: the route people ride. That inverts the model. Nothing below is built. It waits for your yes.

TodayTargetNote
offer nodeline {offer, tier: out | front | core, colour}The inversion. Tier sets the weight. Colour is stored, not picked at draw time.
source nodeoutreach line (tier out) that ends at the hubConflict: today has many source nodes; the map has one hub. Sources become lines into one hub per business.
step nodestation att or timeSet by action: comment, reply, dm, email, text, cta, visit, form are attention. call, book, held are time.
plan nodestation cash that points to an offer or a bundlePrice, packages and plans move onto the offer (section 8). The station stores no price.
offer termspackages, plans, bundles, credits, add-ons, purchasesNew, section 8. A purchase writes the money events.
flow edgetrack {by: system | person, owner?, promise?}Gap: advance[] names the trigger, not who causes it. Owner and promise need new fields. Today's list reads them.
loop edgeexit spur, or a grey line back to the hubA referral is outreach again until the person picks an offer.
nonegroup {id, name, line, stations[]}New, for zoom. One level. The Parts level needs no data: it folds by line and column.
nonecolumn on each station: marketing, sales, delivery, moneyNew. Today the column comes from depth.

What this breaks

  • Depth order. depthOf places nodes today. Columns and line order replace it, in every caller.
  • Journeys pinned to node ids. A migration maps each node id to its new station id. History keeps the old ids.
  • Deals made on offer arrival. A deal now opens at the first station of an offer line.
  • The numbers views. Funnel counts read stations and lines, not nodes.
  • Tokens. Button case, label case and radius change on every page at once. Client looks keep their own presets.

F. Focus view: open one part on its own

Any line, group, station or shared flow opens on its own page at its own URL. The page draws that part only, with faded stubs where people come in and go out. Beside it, what the part holds: SOPs, links, pages, docs, notes, and the apps it uses. A URL per part means ⌘-click opens it in a new window, and a link works in Slack, a note or an SOP. Wren builds no tab bar of its own.

URL/map/line/growth-partner, /map/station/review-callStable ids that survive versions. Browser tabs, windows and history do the isolation.
StubsFaded tracks at the edgesEach stub names where it comes from or goes to. Click one to focus that part instead.
BreadcrumbMap › Growth Partner › Monthly loop › Review callEsc goes up one level.

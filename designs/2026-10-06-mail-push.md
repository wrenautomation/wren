# Mail push into the Watch and the books

2026-10-06. William, via "go on everything": Gmail push into Books, on the Watch's mail path, free tier.

## Now

- The Watch reads William's two inboxes every 15 minutes (`Watch/all`, box).
- The books search billing mail once a day (`Books/all`, box). A bill shows up to a day late.
- Gmail push exists for outreach inboxes only. Pub/Sub topic `gmail-push` in project `wrenautomation`,
  pushed to the phone Worker's `/webhooks/gmail`, then `InboxPush/<address>/notify`, then that
  inbox's `InboxScheduler` pass. An address nothing claimed is taken as an `InboxScheduler` key.

## Change

1. **One receiver, many listeners.** `InboxPush` keeps who listens per address: the `InboxScheduler`
   key (as now) and any loop object that asked through `listen({service, key, until})`. `notify` wakes
   each live one. Nothing claimed: the old fallback, unchanged.
2. **The Watch keeps its own watches.** Each pass renews `users.watch` for a mailbox with under a
   day left, then `listen`s on it. Delegated mailboxes renew through the service account's
   `GmailClient.watch`, on the topic in its own project. A push wakes `Watch/all` (the loop's `wake`).
   While every mailbox is watched, the pass waits 60 minutes, not 15; the poll stays as the net.
3. **Bills ride the Watch's mail events.** The `watch` workflow gets a node `bills` (`books.bills`)
   wired from `read.mail`, next to triage. The step looks up the mail's sender and subject and
   matches them against the books' vendor rules (`vendorFor`). A match sends `Books/all/bills`
   through ingress. Its idempotency key is the mail id, so a retried step sends once.
4. **`Books/all/bills`** runs the books' day without AWS: capture, read, post, alerts. Cost Explorer
   bills per call, so it stays on the daily pass. The daily pass is the net for anything the Watch
   skips (promotions, social).

## Site mailboxes

The personal gmail.com inbox is read through autobrowse's OAuth client. Gmail publishes only to a
topic in the API client's project, and that project is outside william@'s gcloud access. It stays on
the poll until a topic exists there. autobrowse's gmail site gets `POST /gmail/v1/users/me/watch` so
the switch is one setting (`WREN_WATCH_SITE_TOPIC`) when it does.

## Cost

Pub/Sub: the first 10 GB a month are free; a push is about 1 KB. Gmail API calls are free. Model
reads per bill are the same reads the daily pass makes, just earlier.

## Decision log

- Listeners on `InboxPush`, not a second webhook: one Pub/Sub subscription, one door.
- The books' step is thin and hands off to the box. Spine steps run on Lambda, and the books' desk
  and model waits belong on the box (`BOX_SERVICES`).
- The whole day minus AWS, not a one-message capture: it reuses every check, and a search that finds
  nothing new costs one Gmail call.

# comment (comments on our posts, as events)

Every comment on our posts and every answer to our comments. Two sources (`channel`): `reach`, as each reach account's inbox lists it; `content`, as `SocialWatch/wren` reads Wren's own posts on every content platform (designs/2026-10-06-social-inbox.md). A row, then one `comment` event on the `reach.comments` workflow. William answers, DMs or drops each one from Marketing → Inbox; nothing goes out without his click. TikTok comments come from the box's `/web/videos/{id}/comments` (no reply API): our reply made by hand under one marks it answered at the reply's time (`answeredByHand`, `social/store.ts`), so the reply rate counts it; a TikTok post is read every 6 h while young, daily after, and a LinkedIn post every 2 h while young, every 8 h after (both read through a capped page, `EVERY` in `social/store.ts`). A due post is read only if its count moved: each pass asks `Content.commentCounts` once per platform, and a post whose count held since its last full read is skipped until its sweep (6 h young, 24 h older; X and TikTok 12 h / 48 h; `canSkip`, `social/store.ts`; state `counts`, `fullReads`). A pushed post, a failed or empty count read, or a post the count leaves out is read as before (designs/2026-10-15-fetch-budget.md). A used daily cap (X search, 50 a day) holds that platform's comment reads, or its count probe, until it resets: state `capHolds`, said in `stats.held`, no error; the held posts stay due (`holdCap`, `@wren/core/content`, 2026-10-15). Every read waits at most its limit (5 min, `SocialInbox.read` 10; `LIMITS`, `@wren/core/restate`): one that gives no answer is cancelled and its post skipped; a platform that times out twice in a pass waits for the next; a post that times out 3 passes in a row is held (stage `loop.social-watch/<key>`, [[platform/checks]]). `ReachWatch` the same: every desk read 10 min, units per account read (`replies|comments|health|threads <account>`), grouped by platform, stage `loop.reach-watch/<key>`.

## Why this shape

Comments work like a webhook (William, 2026-10-05): the reader is a source, reach is one subscriber. One inbox read per account serves DMs and comments, and marks nothing read. Reads follow the warm cadence (William, 2026-10-06: "frequent checks at first, then decays"): every 2 minutes for 15 minutes after the account's last touch (a DM either way, a comment, our answer, a post on its platform), every 5 to the hour, every 15 to 6 hours, then every 30, each ±20%. LinkedIn never reads sooner than every 36 minutes (its 48 a day inbox cap, 8 kept spare). A used daily cap is no error: that account's next read waits until the cap resets (`capWaitMs`, `@wren/core/content`, 2026-10-15). A send, an answer or a post wakes the watch. Health stays every 30. Our two Reddit accounts never write in one thread, so an answer reads the thread's authors first.

# spine

The Workflows spine: events move along a workflow's routed wires (`via: "events"`) and run each node's step. Tables `events` (every arrival), `hooks` (the door's webhooks) and `workflow_saves` (wiring saved on the canvas); Restate service `Spine`.

## Why this shape

A workflow is data (`packages/core/src/workflows.ts`), so one walker runs any of them. One row per workflow, node path, port and subject means nothing enters a node twice. The row's `by` is the Restate call that kept it, so a retried step runs again instead of skipping. A timed wait is that row with `due` plus a delayed `Spine/release`; nothing polls. A step that fails past 3 tries keeps its `error` on the row and the walk goes on.

## Shape

- `events`: `uq_events_entry (workflow, node, port, subject)`; `node` is dotted from the top workflow ("warm.follow"), "out" for its own output. In main and in every client's database.
- `hooks`: main only; `token_hash` (sha256 of a 43-char token), `client` (null is Wren), `workflow`, `input`, `subject` (the payload field, dotted), `fields` (a field map: name, phone, email, consent, source, zone, niche, each a dotted payload path; `wren hooks add --field`). `HOOK_PRESETS` (`door.ts`) are known senders' subject and map: `site` is the lander (`wren hooks preset site`), subject `id` (`site:<table>:<row id>`), so a resent row claims the same entry and enters once; it goes in the lander's Pages secret `WREN_DOOR_URL`. `Spine/hook` puts `leadOf(payload, fields)` on the event as `data.lead` (`packages/core/src/door.ts`). `open` (default true): a shut door answers 409 and counts the call; a template's door is shut until its install goes live. `sealed`: the token AES-256-GCM sealed with `WREN_HOOK_KEY` (`doors.ts`), so `manage` can see it again; without the key it is shown once.
- `Spine/emit` (private): events leaving `node.port` or `in.port`. With `onlyLive` (`EmitRequest`) it does nothing unless the client's install of that workflow is `live` (`liveFor`), and nothing for Wren; triggers that feed templates (call events, call outcomes) emit freely this way ([[sms/missed-call]], [[sms/review-ask]]). `Spine/release` (private, delayed): a wait is over. `Spine/retry` (private): a failed arrival runs again for the call that took it, its error cleared. `Spine/hook` (public): the phone Worker's door. `Spine/fire` (private): a reply or booking to every live trigger node that hears it, skipping a client's template workflows that aren't live (`notLive`). `SpineClock/start|stop|tick` (private): a Schedule node's clock.
- `spine_events` (view): every arrival with `state` failed, waiting or passed. Record `console.event`, the Workflows app's Events page; Retry is `ConsolePortal/retryEvent` (`wren:effect`), which waits on `Spine/retry` and says "Ran again" or "Failed again". Main only.
- `events.sent`, `events.sent_at`: what the arrival's step sent on (`[{port, subject, kind, data}]`, data dropped past 32 KB but a follow-up's `data.follow` kept, `sentOf`), written by `SpineStore.sent` inside the same journaled step. `[]`: the step sent nothing (its own code moves it). Null: not kept (older rows, a wait).
- `spine_executions` (view): one row per workflow and subject. `state` failed, waiting or done; `node` is where it is now (the failed node, else the waiting one, else the newest); `entered`, `last_at`, `steps`; `title` names who and what from the subject ("Dana Lee: Text lead", a mail's sender and subject, "Dana Lee: DM lead", "Email (gone)" when its row is gone; migrations 0153, 0159). Record `console.execution` (`executionRecordOf`): "Now at" names a step inside a part in words (`nowAtLabels`: "Follow-up: after DM 1"); its `load` is every step with in and out data (`executionSteps`, `console.ts`). Main only.
- Steps register by part id or custom step name in the worker, and get `{client, workflow, node, with, template, part}` (`with`: the node's settings; `template`: the node's copy; `part`: the part it runs inside, `partAt`). Registered: `sms.touch`, `reach.touch`, `watch.triage` ([[watch/mail]]), `sms.forms` and `voice.call_now` (speed to lead, [[sms/speed-run]]), `calls.brief` ([[email/call-brief]]), `setup.step` ([[platform/account-setup]]). A booked call enters `close` at `in.calls`; its outcome leaves by `outcome.won` (and enters `onboarding` at `in.clients`) or `outcome.later` ([[email/call-booking]]). A node with no step keeps the arrival and stops.
- Follow-ups: `cadenceWorkflow` (`workflows.ts`) makes a cadence a workflow `follow_up.<name>` of touch nodes `s<n>`, waits on the wires. Each text sequence is one (`textCadence`, `packages/channel-sms/src/follow.ts`), and each DM sequence (`reachCadence`, `packages/outreach/src/follow.ts`; a client's `reach.touch` runs in its own database). A part's own code emits a node's output with `spineEmit`: `SmsSender` and `ReachSender` send `s<n>.sent` for every step they sent, so a wait counts from the send, not the queue. A custom step at an https URL is POSTed `{port, event}` and answers `{out}`.
- Follow-up and Nurture (`packages/core/src/follow.ts`, design `designs/2026-10-07-follow-up-nurture.md`): parts whose `inside` is `follow_up.touches` / `nurture.touches`, touch nodes with no `with.step` (`isFollowTouch`) between Waits until an answer. `followStart` gates each touch: answered or booked anywhere → `replied`; an active sequence, the part's settings, no thread, the global gate or the client's `sends` flag → passes on with `data.follow` (`did` queued, would_send, skipped, answered). Texts and DMs queue kind `follow_up`; the Email step only checks for an answer. The canvas opens a part's inside through the part (`openedBy`, path `keep_warm/follow_up`): the part's detail carries `inside` plus the editor fields, so the inside edits and saves like a workflow; a step inside a part lights its card (`dotsOf`).
- `workflow_saves`: main only; one row per save from the canvas, `client` (null is Wren), `workflow`, `edits` (`WorkflowEdits`: the routed wires and custom steps, whole; null is back to the code's), `live`, `by`, `at`. `live = false` is the draft: at most one per client and workflow, newer than the live one. The newest live row runs; older live rows are History (`workflowHistory`). `flowsWith` (`workflows.ts`) merges a save over the code's nodes and built-in wires and checks it. A save that stops passing after a code change is skipped, and the canvas says why. The spine reads the client's live saves once per `emit` or `release` call, journaled.
- Versions: `events.version` is the wiring a subject entered on (a live save's id, 0 the code's, null from before versions). `walk` groups a batch by `SpineStore.entered` and walks each group on its own wiring (`Walk.liveOf`, `Walk.flowsAt`, which reads `savedVersion`); a resume or retry walks its arrival's `version`. New subjects take the live one.
- Drafts: `ConsolePortal/workflowSave` (`wren:manage`) keeps the draft as it reads and returns what won't run (it refuses only what doesn't parse: a built-in wire, a custom step not at an https URL). `workflowPublish` checks it as the spine would (`checkWorkflows`, `logicProblems`), then a workflow whose parts send needs `wren:effect`, one that spends `wren:money`, and either needs its id typed back (`confirm`): William's yes. It inserts a live row and drops the draft. `workflowDiscard` drops it. A client gets only workflows for clients. A client's workflow from an installed template doesn't go live here: publish asks in To approve (`askTemplate`), and a yes makes the draft live, opens the door and starts its loops.
- Ask Claude on the graph: `workflowAsk` opens a `record-ask` run (`argv.record = "console.workflow"`, prompt from `workflow-ask.ts`) and sends `Ask/edit`; `workflowAnswer` polls it. The patch is the whole next draft; the canvas shows it as a diff and Accept makes it the draft.
- Made workflows (designs/2026-10-09-workflow-builder.md): id `made.<slug>` (`isMade`, `madeIdOf`), no code; the whole graph is its `workflow_saves`, each save's edits carrying `made {name, blurb, for}`. `flowsWith` reads one as `madeBase` (no ports or nodes) plus its edits; a broken save runs as the blank base. `madeWorkflows(db, client)` lists them with live/draft and node count; `dropMade` deletes drafts, refused while live (`MadeLive`). Console `workflowBuild {client?, message?}` saves an empty draft and, with a message, opens the same Ask run (the prompt says start with a trigger); `workflowsMade`, `workflowDelete`. Rename rides `workflowSave.name`. Reset, Discard before first publish, and Save as template are refused for made ids. Team only. Portal: "Made here" on the Workflows root (`modules/wren/made.tsx`); the canvas opens with `?edit=1` and a pending `?ask=`.
- Test (dry): `Walk.dry` swaps every node's step; `dry.ts` (`dryWalk`, `dryStep`) walks on a store in memory and a clock of its own, follows code wires, and stubs what sends, spends or posts ("Would send"). `ConsolePortal/workflowTest` (`wren:run`) tests the draft as sent, from an input or a trigger's output, or one node. Nothing is claimed or called.
- Logic nodes and triggers (`packages/core/src/logic.ts`, pure): `logic.if` (asks the rule model), `logic.switch` (a field's value picks the case port, else `other`), `logic.wait` (a time: the walker holds what leaves it, `holdOf`; until an event: below), `logic.split` (FNV-1a of node and subject, so a subject lands the same side every time), `logic.merge` (both inputs claim port `in`, so a subject passes once), `trigger.hook` (the door enters at the node: `hookEvent` sends from `<node>.out`). A Webhook node's field map is its `map.<fact>` settings (`fieldMapOfWith`). Publish makes one door per client, workflow and node (`ensureDoors`, input = node id), kept across publishes, its subject and map synced; `workflowPublish` returns tokens it made. `workflowDoors` (read) lists them masked; `doorReveal` and `doorRotate` need `wren:manage`. A test from a door node reads the post by its map and returns `entered`. Ports follow the node's `kind` setting. `trigger.form` is a door too: `form` any (subject its mapped email) or a `HOOK_PRESETS` key (that preset's subject and map). `trigger.reply` (`channel` any|email|sms|dm) and `trigger.booking` (`on` booked|cancelled|any) are fired: a channel calls its `fire` dep (`spineFire`, worker only) and the private `Spine/fire` sends the event to each live node that hears it (`triggerHears`, `hearersOf`). Reply subjects are the thread (`replyFired`: `reply:sms:<contact>`, `reply:reach:<contact>`, `reply:email:<enrollment>`), so a lead enters once; a STOP never fires. Fired from `SmsEvents/ingest(For)` (`ApplyResult.replied`), `InboxScheduler` (`SyncStats.replied`), `ReachWatch` (`RepliesStats.replied`), `CallBookings` (`bookingFired`). `trigger.schedule` (`every` day at `at` in `zone`, default America/New_York since clients keep no zone, or every `hours` on the hour UTC; `nextSlot`) runs on `SpineClock`, a virtual object per `<client>|<workflow>|<node>` (`clockKey`): `start` keeps the next slot and sends itself `tick` delayed; a tick whose slot isn't kept is stale; a tick with no live node stops. Publish and a template approve start its clocks (`clocksOf`, as loop keys); uninstall stops them. Each slot enters once (`item:schedule:<node>@<slot>`). Steps come from `logicSteps(rule)` under the worker's own.
- Template install ([[platform/template-store]] for its copy; designs/2026-10-07-template-install.md, `packages/core/src/template-install.ts`): a workflow with `template {parts, copy, door}` installs onto a client in one transaction: each part through `installCheck` (`installs.ts`), copy as defaults, a draft save, a shut door. `workflow_installs {client, template, version, state draft|waiting|live|off, applied {added, blocks, copy, kept}, hook}` (main only). `planOf` is the diff shown first (new, same, update, back); a block or word the client changed is kept. Missing accounts and facts read "Needs your account" and never block. `uninstallTemplate` stops loops, shuts the door, keeps data, words and saves. Shop Templates section, template page with the plan, the client's Templates; `wren workflows install|uninstall|plan`. Save as template (`workflow-templates.ts`, `workflowTemplateSave`, `wren:manage`; the editor's History menu; `wren workflows save-template`): a client workflow's newest live save kept in `workflow_templates {id saved_<name>, name, blurb, workflow, edits, from_client, by, at}` (main only, migration 0157); the same name again moves it. `templatesNow(db)` = the code's `templatesOf` then `savedTemplatesOf`: a saved one is its code workflow rewired by `edits`, that workflow's template parts and copy (installed as `of`, the code template, so `comesWith` holds), and every effect its wiring has, so approve keeps the sends and spends gate. Install drafts `edits`; a client's workflow follows one template (`installOnWorkflow`, else 409). One whose workflow left the code or whose wiring broke isn't sold. Delete (`deleteWorkflowTemplate`, `workflowTemplateDelete`, `wren:manage`; Library > Workflows and the Marketplace page, Wren's team; `wren workflows delete-template <id|name>`): refused while any client's install is `live`, saying how many, never who; audited by the row trigger under the deleter. Installs not live keep their drafts; approving one then fails (`templateNamed`).
- Auto-retry and Replay (designs/2026-10-07-webhooks-out.md): `WorkflowEdits.retry` (0 off to 5) is set on the editor bar and saved with the draft. A step that fails past its 3 tries calls `Walk.failed`, and `walkFor` sends a delayed `Spine/retry {auto: n+1}` on `RETRY_LADDER_MS` (5m, 30m, 1h, 3h, 6h) while `n` is under the workflow's tries (`packages/core/src/spine.ts:967`). `ConsolePortal/replay` (`wren:effect`, `console.ts:3492`) takes up to 200 event ids or `workflow/subject` executions, finds their failed steps (`failedOf`, `spine.ts:488`) and sends `Spine/retry` for each; the Replay action on Executions and Events (bulk), and "Replay all" on a canvas's Failed tab. `failedRuns` (`:467`) counts failed subjects per workflow with the top error, for the daily digest ([[clients/client-flag]]); partial index `ix_events_failed`.
- Publishing: `SpineDeps.publish` (worker: `webhooksPublish`) sends `Webhooks/publish` for the door (`lead.created`, `form.submitted`), `Spine/fire` (`reply.received`, `booking.made`, `booking.cancelled`) and `close`'s `outcome.won` (`deal.won`). [[platform/webhook-subscription]]. `logic.webhook` (Send webhook, group `action`) is a logic node with a real step; dry tests stub it ("Would POST to host").
- Wait until an event: `logic.wait` with `mode: "until"`, `until` (reply, booking, cancelled, answer: a reply or a booking, `untilsFreedBy`) and `most` ("3 days"; `untilOf`). Out `out` (it happened, `data.happened` is that event) and `timeout`. The walker holds the subject on the node's `in` row with `events.until` and `events.about` (`aboutOf`: the subject past its kind, lower case, so `lead:sms:42` and `reply:sms:42` are both `sms:42`; migration 0158, partial index `ix_events_until`) and a delayed `Spine/release` at its most. `Spine/fire` looks up held rows by what fired (`untilOfFacts`) and `aboutsOf` (the reply's thread; a booking's call, `email:<enrollment>` and booker email, from `bookingFired`) plus every other thread of the same lead (`leadThreads`, `packages/core/src/leads.ts`, through `lead_channels`), and sends `Spine/release {happened}`. Release takes the row once (`due IS NOT NULL`), so the first wins and a late event or a late most does nothing. A wire's own "until" wait still refuses (`waitMs`). Dry test: times out unless `heard` names it. `spine_events.until`, `spine_executions.until` say what a held subject waits for.

## Connected to

- **owns:** `events` (incl. `version`), `hooks` (incl. `open`), `workflow_saves` (incl. `live`), `workflow_installs`, `workflow_templates`, views `spine_events`, `spine_executions`
- **owned-by:** restate-services
- **feeds:** [[platform/webhook-subscription]] (events published to clients' URLs)
- **joins:** workflows by id (code, not a table); `hooks.client` and `workflow_saves.client` name `clients.id`
- **looks-like-but-is-not:** `run_events` (a run's feed lines), `sms_events` (Telnyx deliveries)

## If you change this

- **Hits:** every workflow with routed wires, and every saved copy of it (a removed node or port makes a save fail its check, so the built-in wiring runs); renaming a node or port strands waiting rows (release fails terminal) and old arrivals stop counting. Renaming a text sequence or its steps strands its cadence's waits the same way. Wherever `SmsSender` or `ReachSender` runs, `Spine` must be bound: their sends go there.
- **Does not hit:** wires `via: "code"`; those parts move work themselves.

# portal-shell

The portal's frame: Today at "/", one sidebar with every app under its area, and the open app inside the window: a bar across its top and its pages down its left edge (designs/2026-10-10-portal-areas.md, designs/2026-10-11-portal-look.md). Reshaped 2026-10-19 (designs/2026-10-19-portal-reshape.md): Wren's team sees Primary, Marketing and Tools; a client sees the areas it had.

## Why this shape

Areas group apps by job. A page that moves between apps, or an app that gets a new id, leaves an entry in `MOVED` (`moved.ts`), so every link in a ping, a mail or a brief keeps working. The sidebar never unfolds, so it keeps one shape from app to app. One read of every app's waiting counts feeds the sidebar, the dock and Today's Move list, so they never disagree.

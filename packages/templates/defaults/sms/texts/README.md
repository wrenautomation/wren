# Texts

Default copy for texts. Each file is one text. They go only to people who asked to be texted,
called the business, or just bought from it. First texts say how to opt out.

- `follow-up#1`, `follow-up#2`: Follow-up's two texts, a few days apart.
- `nurture#1`, `nurture#2`: Nurture's texts, months 1 and 3.
- `missed-call-new`, `missed-call-known`: the text back after a missed call. New callers get the
  first, which names the business and says how to opt out. People already in your texts get the
  second.
- `review-ask`, `review-reminder`: the review request and its one reminder. Both carry the
  counted link to your Google review form.
- `review-feedback`: one line added to every ask when the private feedback form is on.

# Sound kit: what is in, what needs your pick

## In the kit now

21 CC0 sounds. `wren video sfx list` lists them, and every render plays the ones an edit names.

- Kenney packs (kenney.nl, CC0): click-1..3, key-1..3, pop-1..3, ding-1..3, error-1, glitch-1, hit-1, hit-2.
- Made by `build.sh`: swish-1, whoosh-1, whoosh-2, riser-1, boom-1.

`wren video sounds <id>` puts them on an edit. It adds a click on each Cap click and a key tap on each key press. It adds a pop on each text overlay and a swish on each layout change. Each Short gets a boom under its title card.

## Your picks

1. Music beds. There are none yet. Each free source with no credit line needs an account:
   - YouTube Audio Library: your Google login. Some tracks are for YouTube only.
   - Pixabay Music: a free account. Commercial use, no credit.
   - Freesound: an account and an API key. CC0 tracks only.

   Pick one. I make the account and pull 5 to 10 beds (calm tech, upbeat, tense) into the media bucket.
2. Better whooshes and risers. The made ones are clean but plain. The Sonniss GDC bundles are free, and their licence lets us use them in videos with no credit. It does not let us share the raw files, so they go in the media bucket, never the repo. Yes or no?
3. Paper, camera shutter and a typing bed. The Kenney packs do not have them. I take them from Freesound CC0 if you pick Freesound in 1, or from Sonniss if you say yes to 2.

Mixkit is off the list. Its downloads need a browser session, and I did not check its licence for each file.
